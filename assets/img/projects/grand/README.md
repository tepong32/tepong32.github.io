## Fixture and isolation

A detached checkout of the pinned source ran on loopback with two fresh SQLite
databases. Media, private documents, exports and backups were redirected into
that preview's disposable runtime; dotenv loading and real email delivery were
disabled. Only source-controlled default logo/avatar assets were copied into its
media directory. Existing GRAND checkouts and operational databases were not used
for records or changed.

Finance data was generated by the existing
`VoucherWorkflowTests.setUpTestData` and
`test_authoritative_budget_to_reconciled_treasury_report_replay` service scenario;
its assertions completed during fixture generation. This is not a new full-suite,
native-MySQL, production, or release validation claim. That scenario also exercises
an intentional report-download rejection; the corresponding 404 is expected.

Inventory data was created through `inventory.receiving` and
`inventory.services.create_asset`. Separate synthetic clerk/reviewer accounts
submitted and accepted the receipt, retaining two synthetic PDF fixtures. The
screens show demonstrative quantities and prices, not observed operating metrics.
Accounting classification was deliberately left unreviewed rather than fabricated.

Capture tooling remains local under the isolated worktree's `.tmp/`; authentication
states and databases are excluded from this portfolio. A missing preview default
image was resolved using the source-controlled asset before capture. GSO's existing
favicon 404 has no screenshot effect; no application source was changed to hide it.
