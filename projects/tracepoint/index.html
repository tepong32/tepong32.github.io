The problem

Continuity without unnecessary account friction

Assistance requests move across document submission, review, correction, approval, claiming, and closure. Citizens need a safe way to continue without creating an account, while staff need role-aware actions, valid transitions, and a complete operational history.

SubmitNo citizen account
CompleteSecure edit link
ReviewRole-aware staff flow
FulfillAuditable closure

Why this became its own project

I needed to understand the lifecycle, not just the form

A request form is the easy part. The harder questions begin after submission: who can change the record, what happens when a document is rejected, and how does the citizen continue safely? TracePoint gave me a focused place to work through those rules before carrying the stronger parts into GRAND.

Engineering decisions

Rules live in services, not scattered views

Central lifecycle policy

Request states and transitions share one vocabulary across public and staff flows.

Secure continuation

A dedicated edit token acts as the public mutation credential.

Document separation

Document review states remain independent from request states.

Audit-friendly events

Status, document, citizen, notification, and workflow actions become timeline entries.
