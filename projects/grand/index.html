The problem

One organization, many disconnected workflows

Local-government teams serve citizens while managing employees, departments, leave, records, announcements, and back-office approvals. GRAND explores how those related workflows can share identity, permissions, data, and operational visibility without becoming one tightly coupled feature.

Engineering decisions

Grow by domain, not by one oversized application

Modular Django apps

Users, profiles, leave, assistance, departments, home, salaries, and admin helpers have distinct responsibilities.

Role-aware workflows

Public, employee, HR, department, and administrative actions require different access and interfaces.

Traceable changes

Profile edit logs and assistance update histories preserve operational context.

Incremental delivery

Usable workflows can mature while salary and calendar domains remain explicitly planned work.
