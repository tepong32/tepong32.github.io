The problem

One transaction crosses several offices

A funded request can pass through Budget, Accounting, Treasury, inventory staff, and records custodians. Each office has different authority, but the amount, supporting documents, and transaction history must remain connected. GRAND models those responsibilities as separate Django applications sharing identity and department context.

From office work to software

Built around problems I understand firsthand

Municipal accounting exposed me to repeated encoding, paper handoffs, and records that need to survive review. I started GRAND outside office hours to turn those operating rules into explicit software behavior. It has grown from employee and assistance workflows into Finance, reporting, records, and inventory.

Finance workflows

Follow the source from Budget to Treasury

I develop linked workflows for appropriations, allotments and obligations, payable review, journal posting, disbursement vouchers, payment instruments, and bank reconciliation. Independent preparation and approval steps preserve who proposed a transaction, who authorized it, and which source it used.

BudgetAuthorize and certify funding
AccountingReview payables and post journals
TreasuryRelease and reconcile payments
ReportingTrace outputs to source records

Posted financial history is retained. Corrections use linked reversals and reviewed successors so an amended transaction remains explainable. Reports, ledgers, and exported evidence preserve their source references and checksums.

Inventory and custody

Connect physical assets to accountable records

The integrated General Services Office (GSO) inventory module covers asset registers, receiving and inspection, asset handoffs, consumable requests and issues, physical verification, and reviewed disposal requests. It reuses GRAND's authentication, department membership, and access controls.

Physical-count reconciliation compares the inventory register, observed assets, and retained Accounting CSV evidence. Finance associations have their own review: recording a receipt or moving an asset does not silently post a journal or establish its accounting valuation.

GRAND's separate TracePoint module tracks QR-labelled paper packets and confirmed handovers. Records manages retained files and controlled retrieval, keeping document custody distinct from financial approval.

Engineering decisions

Make authority and data ownership explicit

Separate database responsibilities

A Django database router places Accounting and Budget authority data in the Finance database. Identity, operational cases, inventory, and its Finance adapter remain in the default store. Explicit references and reconciliation connect the two.

Permissions at workflow boundaries

Department scope and role checks apply to actions and downloads. Independent maker-checker review separates preparation from approval; read-only acceptance-test access does not grant financial mutation rights.

History survives corrections

Versioned decisions, immutable posted records, source-linked reversals, and retained export bytes preserve what was approved. Corrections add evidence instead of overwriting the original transaction.

Test recovery and competing actions

I use regression and integration tests to exercise authorization, duplicate prevention, reconciliation, interrupted operations, and native MySQL concurrency. Synthetic transaction replays connect checks across office boundaries.

My work spans requirements, Django models and services, forms and interfaces, database migrations, tests, and release documentation. I use AI-assisted development with source review and validation of generated changes.

Public services and internal operations

Citizen and employee workflows

Account-free assistance submission and tracking, staff document review, social-welfare programs, employee profiles, leave, announcements, and configurable department dashboards share the platform.

Reports and controlled records

Report definitions, scheduled generation, review, approval, retention, and audited downloads support office work. Portable exports retain checksums and source history for transfer through TraceSync.

Development status
