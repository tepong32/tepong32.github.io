About

From operations to software engineering

I am a self-directed Python and Django developer with a BSBA in Management and professional experience across local government, accounting, customer support, accounts payable, and data operations.

I was curious about technology long before I formally started programming in 2017. Open forums showed me how much useful knowledge people share simply because someone else has a problem to solve. That stayed with me: I want the software I build to be useful to somebody, even if the improvement starts small.

I moved into development by building tools around problems I understood firsthand. My current work focuses on Django applications, explicit workflow rules, data integrity, and desktop automation.

I am looking for a backend-focused role where I can contribute production-minded implementation while continuing to learn from an experienced engineering team.
